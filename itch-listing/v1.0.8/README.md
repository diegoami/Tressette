**Description (English, ready to paste):**

Play two-player Tressette against Franco, Valerio, Graziano or Piero. Choose
one of six regional Italian card decks, play a twenty-trick deal, and score
points with cards and declarations. Your opponent plays the final seven tricks
with exact endgame calculation.

- Play in your browser, or download the Android and Windows versions.
- Choose from six decks: Trevisane, Piacentine, Napoletane, Romagnole, Francesi
  and Bresciane.
- Switch the interface and rules between English and Italian.
- No account, ads or online matches. Settings and game history stay on your
  device.
- The Android and Windows versions work offline after installation.

For a better phone experience, play at
[tresettette.netlify.app](https://tresettette.netlify.app). The fullscreen button
on itch.io remains your choice.

### Make the uploaded HTML5 ZIP playable on the page

The `html5` build is already uploaded. Butler cannot enable its browser embed.
In itch.io **Dashboard → Tressettette → Edit game**:

1. Set **Kind of project** to **HTML** (sometimes shown as **HTML game**),
   rather than Downloadable.
2. Under **Uploads**, find the ZIP on the `html5` channel and tick
   **This file will be played in the browser**. Leave that option off for the
   Android APK and Windows EXE; those remain downloadable.
3. Under **Embed options**, choose **Embed in page** and set width **480** and
   height **720**. Keep the fullscreen button available, without forcing it.
4. Save the page and open the private draft preview. It should show a browser
   game window above the Android and Windows downloads.

These are page settings, so re-uploading the same ZIP cannot fix a missing
embed. See the [butler HTML channel instructions](https://itch.io/docs/butler/pushing.html#html-playable-in-browser-games)
and [itch.io HTML5 game guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5).

The HTML5 game also supports 640×480 and other window sizes; 480×720 is the
canonical itch embed. Preview the private draft on a phone before publishing.
